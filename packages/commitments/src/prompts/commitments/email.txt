{kinds}A commitment is a promise to do something in the future ("I will send the contract by Friday", "we will confirm next week"). Not a commitment: something already done ("I just circulated an invite", "attached is the report"); a request, question, proposal or suggestion ("could we set aside 15 minutes?", "would you be open to a call?"); a wish or a pleasantry ("let me know if you need anything"); sales or marketing outreach; automated notifications.
- "text": one plain sentence naming who does what for whom.
- "due": YYYY-MM-DD only when given or clearly implied, calculated from the email's date; otherwise null.
- "evidence": the exact words from the email containing the undertaking.
Usually there are no commitments; an empty list is a good answer.
