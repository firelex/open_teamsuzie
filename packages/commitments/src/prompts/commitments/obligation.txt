Count "owed_to_you" only for an obligation someone takes on towards the user personally that may need chasing: the user is the one who receives the result (send the user a document, sign something the user sent, review the user's draft, introduce the user to someone, come back to the user with an answer). Not "owed_to_you":
- a promise to do something for, or with, someone other than the user, even when the user is copied or it is part of the user's deal or project ("Will will ask WSD to validate their column", "I'll send the draft to the other side", "we will get back to the client");
- someone saying what they are about to do in the next few minutes as part of the exchange ("I'll add you to the thread now", "give me 5 or 10 minutes", "joining now", "I'll call you in a sec"), or a courtesy.
